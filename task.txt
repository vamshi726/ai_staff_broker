# 1. Executive Summary

Revenue leakage is one of the largest hidden financial problems in enterprises.

According to various industry reports, companies lose between **1%–5% of annual revenue** due to:

- Incorrect pricing
- Billing mistakes
- Missed invoices
- Contract violations
- Duplicate discounts
- Duplicate payments
- Missed renewals
- SLA compensation errors

Most companies discover these issues months later during audits.

The goal of this platform is to build an **Autonomous AI Finance Auditor** that continuously monitors enterprise financial data, identifies revenue leakage, explains the root cause, recommends corrective actions, and assists finance teams in recovering lost revenue.

Unlike traditional rule-based systems, this platform uses **Agentic AI** where specialized AI agents collaborate to investigate financial anomalies autonomously.

Because these systems are disconnected, revenue leakage occurs.

Examples:

- Invoice never generated
- Customer charged incorrect price
- Wrong tax calculation
- Duplicate discounts
- Customer overpaid
- Customer underpaid
- Contract expired but billing continued
- SLA penalties never deducted
- Manual pricing overrides
