# AI Staff Broker — Non-Technical User Guide

Welcome to **AI Staff Broker**! This guide is written in plain English for business owners, managers, supervisors, and workers. No programming knowledge is required.

---

## ❓ What Problem Does It Solve?

In places like warehouses, retail stores, factories, construction sites, and hospitals, managers and workers often speak different languages. 

* **The Challenge**: If a manager speaks Hindi, but workers speak Telugu, Tamil, or Kannada, communication breaks down. Translating instructions manually takes too much time, and written lists are hard to follow on a busy floor.
* **Our Solution**: **AI Staff Broker** is a smart voice assistant. A manager speaks an instruction *once* in their native language. The AI automatically:
  1. Understands the instruction.
  2. Splits it into separate, specific tasks.
  3. Finds the best-suited worker based on their skills.
  4. Translates the task text into the worker's native language.
  5. Converts it into a voice message so the worker can listen to it read aloud.

---

## 📖 A Real-World User Story

### The Setup
* **Rajesh** is a warehouse owner in Hyderabad. He preferred speaking **Hindi**.
* **Subbu** is a worker who speaks **Telugu** and is skilled in *cleaning* and *billing*.
* **Prasad** is another worker who speaks **Kannada** and is skilled in *packing* and *delivery*.

### The Action
1. Rajesh opens the app, presses the record button, and says in Hindi:
   > *"Subbu billing desk ko saaf karo aur Prasad rack number do ke boxes ko pack karo."*
   > *(Subbu clean the billing desk, and Prasad pack the boxes on rack number two.)*
2. Rajesh uploads the recording.

### The AI at Work
* The AI transcribes Rajesh's Hindi speech and translates it to English to analyze it.
* It identifies two distinct tasks:
  * **Task 1**: "Clean the billing desk" (Requires skill: *cleaning* / *billing*)
  * **Task 2**: "Pack the boxes on rack 2" (Requires skill: *packing*)
* The AI looks at the active team:
  * It matches **Task 1** to **Subbu** (since his profile matches *cleaning* and *billing*).
  * It matches **Task 2** to **Prasad** (since his profile matches *packing*).
* The AI translates the instructions:
  * Subbu's task is translated to **Telugu**: *"బిల్లింగ్ డెస్క్‌ను శుభ్రం చేయండి"*
  * Prasad's task is translated to **Kannada**: *"ರ್ಯಾಕ್ 2 ರಲ್ಲಿ పెట్టೆಗಳನ್ನು ప్యాక్ చేయండి"*
* The AI generates a natural-sounding audio file for each translation.

### The Result
* **Subbu** receives a notification on his phone in Telugu. He taps the **Play** button and hears: *"బిల్లింగ్ డెస్క్‌ను శుభ్రం చేయండి"* read aloud. He cleans the desk and marks the task as **Complete**.
* **Prasad** receives a notification in Kannada, plays his audio, packs the boxes, and marks his task as **Complete**.
* Rajesh sees both tasks marked as **Completed** on his dashboard in real-time.

## 🚀 How to Use the App (Step-by-Step)

### For Owners and Supervisors (Managers)
1. **Create an Account**: Go to the Sign-up tab, select your preferred language, and create your account.
2. **Setup your Business**: Create an organization (e.g., "Hyderabad Warehouse").
3. **Invite your Team**: You will see a unique **Invite Code** (e.g., `12345`). Share this code with your workers.
4. **Assign Tasks using Voice**:
   - Go to the **Voice & Tasks** tab.
   - Tap **Start Recording** and say your instructions.
   - Tap **Stop Recording** and click **Upload & Process**.
5. **Track Progress**: Monitor the dashboard to see which tasks are assigned, in-progress, or completed.

---

### For Workers
1. **Create an Account**: Go to the Sign-up tab and register.
2. **Join the Team**: Enter the **Invite Code** shared by your manager to join the organization.
3. **Fill out your Profile**:
   - Go to **My Profile**.
   - Select your preferred language (e.g., Telugu, Tamil, Hindi).
   - Add your skill keywords (e.g., `cleaning`, `packing`, `billing`).
   - Turn on the **Available for tasks** switch.
4. **Receive and Complete Tasks**:
   - Go to **My Tasks**.
   - View your tasks translated into your preferred language.
   - Tap the **Play** button to listen to the instruction read aloud.
   - Tap **Start** when you begin, and **Complete** when you finish.
